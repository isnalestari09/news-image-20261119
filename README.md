# Sum up 19 November's world news in one image

Entry for make.fun bounty `news-image-20261119` — $250, 50 places paid.
Deadline: Fri 21 Nov 2026 07:00 WIB (Fri 21 Nov 2026 00:00 UTC).

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes `output/entry.png` (or `/output/entry.png` when that directory exists).
Python 3.13, standard library only, no network, deterministic — two runs
produce identical bytes.

## The image

A 1024x1024 newspaper front page, "THE WORLD TODAY", dated
19 November 2026, No. 19, Page 1. Lead story: the first Trump–Xi–Putin
trilateral summit in Shenzhen, with Ukraine dominating the agenda. Also
covered: the ICJ ruling on the Rohingya genocide case against Myanmar,
unrest in Bangladesh after Yunus's ancestral home was torched, Ukraine
striking the Crimean Bridge a third time in a week, Kuwait repelling an
Iranian attack as the Gulf widens after the US strike on Qeshm, COP31
talks in Antalya, a Van Gogh selling for USD 190.4M, the EU's proposed
EUR 45BN Ukraine loan, North Korea expanding uranium enrichment, the
Anthropic IPO priced at USD 83, and Fiji's state of disaster over an
El Niño drought — plus the markets line. Every story has its own drawn
illustration; no photographs, no ads, no other date.
